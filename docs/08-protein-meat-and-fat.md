# 08 — Protein, meat and fat: the type is the whole story

## Before and after — real paired measurements

Same patients, measured twice.

| Intervention | Before → After | Duration | n |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Nocturnal** 710 kcal supplement — total body protein | **+0.53 kg** | 12 months | 103 randomised |
| The *same* supplement taken in the **daytime** | **no significant change** | 12 months | same trial |
| Exercise + 10 g leucine — 6-minute walk | 365 → **445 m** (p=0.01) | 12 weeks | 8 |
| Exercise + protein — thigh circumference | 41 → **46 cm** (p=0.02) | 12 weeks | 8 |
| Home exercise — thigh muscle index | 0.62 → **0.70 cm/m²** (control fell 0.67 → 0.63) | 12 weeks | 40 |
| Late-evening snack + amino acids, **alcohol-related** cirrhosis — respiratory quotient | 0.79 → **0.82** | 6 months | 91 |
| Protein + HMB — five-times chair-stand | 18.6 → **16.5 s** (within-group only; **no** between-group difference) | 12 weeks | 21 |

**The timing finding is the cheapest intervention available.** Identical food,
identical calories — only the clock changed, and only the night-time arm built protein.

## Targets

- EASL: **1.2–1.5 g protein/kg/day** (1.5 with sarcopenia). Protein restriction in
  hepatic encephalopathy must be avoided.
- ESPEN in cancer: **>1.0 g/kg/day, up to 1.5** where possible.
- For a 70 kg adult that is **84–105 g/day**.

## Meat and fat — where the evidence splits hard

Risk ratios, highest versus lowest intake unless stated.

| Exposure → outcome | Ratio | 95% CI |
|---|---|---|
| **Saturated animal fat → chronic liver disease death** | **3.50×** | 2.48–4.96 |
| Red meat → chronic liver disease death | **2.59×** | 1.86–3.61 |
| Saturated animal fat → liver cancer | **1.87×** | 1.23–2.85 |
| Processed red meat → liver cancer | **1.84×** | 1.16–2.92 |
| Red meat → liver cancer | **1.74×** | 1.16–2.61 |
| Processed meat → liver cancer | 1.20× | 1.02–1.41 |
| Processed meat → all-cause death, per serving/day | 1.15× | 1.11–1.19 |
| Unprocessed red meat → liver cancer | 1.06× | 0.68–1.63 — *not significant* |
| Red meat → liver cancer (largest meta-analysis) | 1.04× | 0.91–1.18 — *not significant* |
| Fish → liver cancer | **0.78×** | 0.67–0.90 |
| White meat → liver cancer | **0.69×** | 0.58–0.81 |
| Poultry → liver cancer | **0.60×** | 0.40–0.90 |
| **White meat → chronic liver disease death** | **0.52×** | 0.39–0.70 |

**Chicken and fish sit on the protective side. Red meat is neutral-to-harmful,
processed meat is clearly harmful, and saturated animal fat is the worst single line
in the table.** Randomised overfeeding trials confirm the mechanism: at matched
calories, saturated fat raised liver fat **+55%** versus **+15%** for unsaturated.

Practical translation: keep the chicken, add oily fish, treat beef as occasional
rather than a staple, and drop "animal fats" as a goal — the fat should come from
olive oil, oily fish and nuts. Protein was never the problem; the fat riding along
with it is.
