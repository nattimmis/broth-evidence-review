# 13 - Bacteriophage therapy, taken seriously

A proposal arrived that phage treatment from Moscow private hospitals could fix stage 4
cancer, together with a Russian popular-science article, *Phages Attack*, as evidence.

The article is real, accurate and worth reading. It describes phage therapy for **wound
and intestinal infection** - the Russian-Finnish War, Stalingrad at roughly 50,000 doses
a day, complete wound sterilisation in 30-40% of patients, and NPO Microgen today making
14 phage products at over a billion packs a year. **It makes no claim about cancer
anywhere in the piece.**

## 1. Phages cannot treat the cancer, and the reason is mechanical

A bacteriophage is a virus that has evolved to hijack **bacterial** cell machinery.
Human cells do not have that machinery. This is a category difference, not an evidence gap.

Checked directly against ClinicalTrials.gov rather than by reading articles about it:

| | Count |
|---|---|
| Registered bacteriophage trials worldwide | **121** |
| ...that test a phage against a tumour | **0** |
| Peer-reviewed human studies reporting tumour response to phage | **0** |

The eight records that intersect "cancer" are: a bladder-cancer **detection** biosensor;
SNIPR001, whose registered condition is *"E Coli Infections"* (preventing infection in
transplant patients); a record titled *"Phage II Trial of Stathmin"* - a **typo for Phase
II**, an ordinary chemotherapy trial; and five unrelated false matches. Anyone searching
"phage cancer trial" lands on the typo and concludes the field exists.

## 2. The three sciences it gets confused with

| Field | What it is | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Phage display** | Nobel Prize 2018. Phages display peptide libraries so binders can be selected **in a test tube**. Produced adalimumab and necitumumab | A discovery platform. **Never administered to a patient** |
| **Phages vs intratumoural bacteria** | Tumours contain bacteria - 86/113 (76%) of human pancreatic tumours - and those bacteria degrade gemcitabine. Phage-guided nanoparticles deplete them | **Mouse and piglet.** And the goal is helping chemotherapy work, not killing cancer |
| **Oncolytic virotherapy** | Viruses that **do** infect human cells, engineered to prefer cancerous ones. T-VEC approved for melanoma; teserpaturev in Japan | Real and approved - **but not phage therapy.** A herpes virus and a bacteriophage are different classes of thing |

Even T-VEC's phase 3 (n=436) is worth reading carefully: durable response **16.3% vs
2.1%**, but median survival **23.3 vs 18.9 months, p=0.051 - not significant**.

## 3. The closest real thing was tested in liver cancer and made survival worse

**PHOCUS**: phase 3, randomised, advanced hepatocellular carcinoma, **n=459**. Oncolytic
virus (pexa-vec) plus sorafenib versus sorafenib alone. Median overall survival **12.7
months versus 14.0** - the virus arm did worse, and the trial was **terminated early for
futility**.

This used a virus that genuinely infects human cells, far more plausible than a phage,
in exactly this cancer. Plausibility is not efficacy.

## 4. Where phages genuinely could help - the affirmative answer

People with cirrhosis die of infection, and increasingly of resistant infection.

- Hospitalised decompensated cirrhosis: **46.6% (82/176)** had a bacterial infection.
  In-hospital mortality **37.8% infected versus 3.2% uninfected** - a twelvefold difference.
- Spontaneous bacterial peritonitis in-hospital mortality **43.8%** (n=112).
- **34% of infections in cirrhosis are multi-drug-resistant** (n=1,302, 46 centres, four
  continents); a 2026 meta-analysis puts it at **36.7%** (29 studies, 13,361 patients).

Against that, phage therapy's human record: **100 consecutive personalised cases** across
35 hospitals in 12 countries - clinical improvement **77.2%**, target eradication **61.3%**.

**The two honest brakes, stated as loudly as the good numbers:**

- Phage therapy's **only two randomised trials both failed**. In bladder infection after
  surgery, phage cleared **18% versus 28% for placebo** - not superior to placebo. In
  infected burns (PhagoBurn), phage was **slower** than standard care (144h vs 47h) and
  the trial was stopped for insufficient efficacy.
- Every impressive figure comes from uncontrolled case series with obvious selection and
  publication bias, and **eradication was about 70% less likely without concurrent
  antibiotics**. Phages work *with* antibiotics, not instead of them.

### The liver-specific research is not ready

A 2019 *Nature* paper found cytolysin-producing *Enterococcus faecalis* kills liver cells
and correlates with mortality in alcoholic hepatitis; phages targeting it **abolished
ethanol-induced liver disease in humanised germ-free mice**. The authors said a clinical
trial was required.

**Then the human premise weakened.** A Danish prospective cohort (n=28) found only
**3/28 (10.7%)** were cytolysin-positive and **no mortality difference**. Real idea, real
journal, not a treatment that exists for a human today.

### The precondition

Phage therapy is **organism-specific** - a phage is matched to a bacterium cultured from
a particular patient. Without a positive culture naming a resistant organism there is
nothing to match. Every legitimate route (UK unlicensed named-patient supply, Belgium's
magistral framework, compassionate use) is **initiated by the treating doctor**. Scale
check: **12 patients in the entire UK** received phage therapy in the two years to 2023.

**So the honest sequence is: if he develops an infection, insist on cultures; if
something grows that resists the available antibiotics, that is the moment to raise
phage therapy with the infection team. Not before.**

## 5. What Russia has actually announced

- **Microgen's 14 registered phage products** - every indication a bacterial infection.
  No cancer indication in the catalogue.
- **No Moscow clinic was found offering phage therapy for cancer.** The major providers
  offer conventional oncology; the state cancer centre's own vaccinotherapy page lists
  oncolytic viruses, mRNA and dendritic-cell vaccines and **does not mention phages**.
- **NeoOncovac** (Gamaleya mRNA vaccine): authorised for clinical use 21 Nov 2025, first
  patient 1 Apr 2026 - for inoperable or metastatic **cutaneous melanoma**, not liver
  cancer. No phase 3, no peer-reviewed publication, and **zero** Gamaleya oncology
  studies registered on ClinicalTrials.gov.
- **EnteroMix**: only registered trial is **NCT07584668, Phase 1, n=24**, primary endpoint
  the quantity of virus in peripheral blood. No results posted. The "100% efficacy"
  headlines were animal data and were rated unsupported by independent fact-checkers.
- **Practical obstacles**: the FCDO advises against all travel to Russia, **travel
  insurance may be invalidated**, and **Visa/Mastercard/Amex cards issued outside Russia
  do not work there**.

## 6. The measured cost of going this route

The harm is quantified, and it is not what people expect:

- Alternative medicine used **instead of** conventional treatment for curable cancers:
  hazard ratio for death **2.50** (breast 5.68, colorectal 4.57, lung 2.17).
- Complementary-medicine users **refused chemotherapy at 34.1% versus 3.2%**; five-year
  survival 82.2% versus 86.6%, and the excess deaths were **mediated by that refusal**.

**The mechanism of harm is the delay, not the alternative therapy.**

## 7. The lever nobody is selling

Every first-line trial in advanced liver cancer enrolled **Child-Pugh A patients only**.

| | Child-Pugh A | Child-Pugh B |
|---|---|---|
| Tumour response to first-line immunotherapy | **58.3%** | **11.1%** |
| Median overall survival | not reached | **7.7 months** |

In one cohort, immunotherapy in Child-Pugh B was **worse than the older drug** it
replaced (OS 8.2 vs 13.8 months, adjusted HR 2.01). At mALBI grade 3, median survival
was **5.43 months**.

**And liver function in alcohol-related cirrhosis is reversible.** Among 204 abstinent
patients with *decompensated* alcohol-related cirrhosis, **18.1% recompensated**, with an
adjusted hazard ratio for liver-related death of **0.091** - a reduction of over 90%.

Abstinence plus hepatology optimisation is the intervention that can move someone from
the excluded column to the eligible one, where first-line survival is 19-24 months.
**No clinic anywhere is offering a bigger swing, and it costs nothing.**

Meanwhile the standard of care moved: **CheckMate 9DW** (nivolumab + ipilimumab, n=668)
gave median OS **23.7 versus 20.6 months**, HR 0.79, p=0.018 - worth checking the
treating team is working from current numbers. And early palliative care alongside
oncology improved quality of life, halved depression, and lengthened survival
(**11.6 versus 8.9 months**).
