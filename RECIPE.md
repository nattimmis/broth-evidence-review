# Thirteen-Hour Golden Bone Broth

Liver-safe. Makes ~3 litres. Plain ASCII below for copy-paste into a message.

Thirteen hours is legitimate for beef bones — they genuinely reward 12–18h. Two
changes from the original brief: a **bare simmer, not a boil** (a rolling boil
emulsifies the fat and gives cloudy, greasy broth), and **no salt in the pot**.

```
╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║              THIRTEEN-HOUR GOLDEN BONE BROTH                         ║
║              gentle on the liver · makes ~3 litres                   ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

BONES  (2 kg total — the ratio is what makes it set)
  1.2 kg  marrow bones, cut crosswise
  0.8 kg  knuckle, oxtail or short rib   <- the collagen; don't skip
  4 L     cold water
  2 tbsp  apple cider vinegar

ADDED IN THE LAST 90 MINUTES ONLY
  2       onions, halved, skins on (colour)
  2       carrots  ·  2 celery stalks  ·  1 head garlic, halved
  30 g    fresh turmeric, sliced   (or 2 tsp ground)
  1       CEYLON cinnamon stick    <- NOT cassia. This matters.
  1       bay leaf · parsley stems · 6 peppercorns

SALT
  None. Not one pinch. Salt goes in the bowl, at the table.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
METHOD
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 1. Roast bones at 200°C / 425°F for 40 min, until deep brown.
    This is where the flavour comes from. Pale bones = weak broth.

 2. Bones into a tall pot. Cold water to cover by 5 cm. Vinegar.
    Let it sit off the heat 30 minutes.

 3. Bring to a bare simmer SLOWLY — take 45 minutes to get there.

 4. Skim the grey foam for the first hour, patiently.
    ** This single step separates good broth from muddy broth. **

 5. Hold 13 hours at 85–95°C. One lazy bubble every second or two.
    If it rolls, it's too hot. Top up with BOILING water so the
    bones stay covered.

 6. At the 11.5 hour mark, add the aromatics, turmeric, cinnamon.
    Vegetables cooked 13 hours go bitter and muddy. 90 min is plenty.

 7. Strain through a fine sieve lined with muslin.
    Do NOT press the solids — pressing makes it cloudy.

 8. Chill uncovered until cold, then cover. Lift off the fat cap.

 9. It should set to a firm, wobbly jelly. That's the collagen.
    No set = you needed more knuckle and joint next time.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
AT THE BOWL
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Reheat a mug. Then, to taste:
    · lemon juice, fresh herbs, a little garlic  <- flavour without salt
    · 1 tsp olive oil + a normal grind of pepper (culinary amounts are
      fine — it's concentrated piperine supplements that are implicated)
    · salt LAST, and sparingly

  KEEPS: 5 days refrigerated · 6 months frozen
  Freeze in ice-cube trays — one cube is a single dose of comfort.
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```

## Making it actually nourishing

Broth alone is not a protein source (see
[`docs/02-cirrhosis-nutrition.md`](docs/02-cirrhosis-nutrition.md)). Use it as a
carrier for calories and protein someone with no appetite can get down:

| Add | Why |
|-----|-----|
| Shredded chicken or beef | Complete protein, familiar, easy to swallow |
| An egg stirred through, avgolemono-style | Complete protein, thickens, mild |
| Red lentils cooked in it | Protein + calories, breaks down to a smooth body |
| A scoop of complete/whey protein, off the boil | Fixes the tryptophan and BCAA gap directly |
| Olive oil, a swirl at the bowl | Dense calories without volume |

Small volumes, often, beat one large bowl — appetite is usually the binding
constraint, not access to food.

## The Mediterranean sides, adapted

Good pattern, two edits:

- **Skip the salt bombs** — olives, capers, feta, anchovies, preserved lemon.
  Build flavour from lemon, olive oil, herbs, garlic instead.
- **No raw shellfish. Hard rule.** See [`docs/03-food-safety.md`](docs/03-food-safety.md).

Tomato, cucumber, red onion, flat parsley, mint, chickpeas, toasted seeds, good
olive oil, plenty of lemon. Wash produce thoroughly.
